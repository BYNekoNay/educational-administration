# 摘  要

随着艺术培训行业的快速发展，培训机构普遍面临课程类型多、上课频次高、教师排班复杂、学员请假调课频繁、课时扣减与薪资核算依赖人工等管理难题。传统 Excel 或纸质登记方式数据分散、统计滞后，易出现课时误扣、排课冲突与薪资错误，制约机构规模化运营。

针对上述问题，本文设计并实现了一个面向艺术培训机构的全流程教务管理平台。系统采用前后端分离的 B/S 架构，管理后台基于 Vue 3 与 Element Plus 构建，移动端基于 uni-app 实现，后端采用 Spring Boot 3.2.5 与 MyBatis-Plus 3.5.7 提供统一业务接口，数据存储于 MySQL 8.0。系统覆盖招生报名、课程班级管理、智能排课与调课、课堂考勤、课时自动扣减、收费退费、薪资核算与运营统计的完整业务闭环，支持五类角色协同工作，并以版本化迁移等手段保障系统可靠性。

系统采用 JUnit 5、Mockito 与 Vitest 建立分层测试体系，后端 464 个单元测试、管理端 118 个与移动端 25 个测试全部通过，并经 69 条浏览器自动化验收用例确认五类角色主要流程可用。系统已完成容器化生产部署并稳定运行，测试与运行结果表明其功能和并发控制可靠、权限隔离正确，能够满足艺术培训机构数字化教务管理需求。

**关键词**：教务管理，智能排课，课时管控，薪资核算，流失预警

# ABSTRACT

With the rapid development of the art training industry, training institutions generally face management difficulties such as numerous course types, high class frequency, complex teacher scheduling, frequent student leave and class-adjustment requests, and lesson deduction and salary accounting relying on manual work. Traditional Excel or paper-based registration leads to scattered data and delayed statistics, and is prone to lesson deduction errors, scheduling conflicts and salary mistakes, which restricts the scaled operation of institutions.

To address these problems, this paper designs and implements a full-process educational administration management platform for art training institutions. The system adopts a front-end and back-end separated B/S architecture. The management console is built with Vue 3 and Element Plus, the mobile terminal is implemented with the uni-app cross-platform framework, and the back end uses Spring Boot 3.2.5 and MyBatis-Plus 3.5.7 to provide unified business APIs, with data stored in MySQL 8.0. The system covers a complete business closed loop including enrollment and payment, course and class management, intelligent scheduling and adjustment, class attendance, automatic lesson deduction, charges and refunds, salary accounting, and operational statistics, supporting collaboration among five roles: parents, teachers, educational administrators, financial administrators and super administrators. The reliability of the system is guaranteed by versioned database migration, encrypted backup, release gates and business observability.

A layered testing system is established with JUnit 5, Mockito and Vitest. All 464 back-end unit tests, 118 admin-web tests and 25 mobile tests pass, and 69 browser-automation acceptance cases confirm that the main processes of the five roles are usable. The system has been deployed and running in a containerized production environment with stable operation. Test and operation results show that its functions and concurrency control are reliable and permission isolation is correct, satisfying the digital educational administration needs of art training institutions.

**Keywords**: Educational Administration, Intelligent Scheduling, Lesson Management, Salary Accounting, Churn Warning
